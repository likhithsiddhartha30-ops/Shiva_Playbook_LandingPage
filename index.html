The pattern

Pressure gets you moving. Comfort sends you back.

You work well when deadlines are close or pressure builds. When things become comfortable, you return to familiar routines.

You research, plan, organise and stay busy, while the uncomfortable actions that could create new opportunities get pushed aside.

Income Ceiling Breakthrough helps you change that pattern, without depending on motivation or waiting for panic to force you into action.

What we'll work on

Five frameworks for consistent execution

Each one targets a different point where your next level gets stalled, from the identity that pulls you back to the hour you lose to busywork.

01

Identity Thermostat

Identify the habits, beliefs and reactions that keep pulling you back into familiar patterns. Understand how your current self-image influences your actions, and begin aligning your behaviour with your next level of responsibility.

02

Imagination Protocols

Mentally rehearse how you want to respond before difficult moments arrive, and practise the moments you've been avoiding.

The sales conversationSubmitting the proposalAsking for the promotionThe task you keep avoiding
03

Execution Awareness

Learn to recognise resistance as it happens. Notice when you're slipping into avoidance, work through the discomfort, and take the next useful action without waiting to feel motivated.

04

Focus Flow Installation

Build a repeatable way to direct your attention to one important task at a time. Use focused work sessions, clear finish lines and distraction management to turn intentions into completed work.

05

Focus Funnel

Separate the actions that could advance your income goal from routine responsibilities, low-value busywork and distractions. Make deliberate decisions about where your time and attention go.

What you're working towards

By the end of the programme, the aim is for you to:

  1. Execute important actions more consistently, without depending on motivation.
  2. Stop using pressure as your primary trigger for performance.
  3. Prioritise work that supports your income goal instead of hiding in busywork.
  4. Act with greater confidence when outcomes are uncertain.
  5. Build self-trust by following through on commitments.
  6. Recognise and pursue opportunities you might previously have hesitated to take.

Questions

Before you book

Who is this for?

High performers who've already proven they can work hard and get results, but who rely on deadlines and pressure to act, and keep putting off the uncomfortable actions that would take them to their next income level.

How does the programme run?

Over seven weeks you get personal 1:1 coaching sessions on Google Meet, weekly Q&A sessions, the Self-Mastery Playbook and personal accountability on the actions you commit to.

What happens on the call?

It's a 45-minute conversation about where you are, the pattern that keeps repeating and your next income goal. If the programme is a fit, we'll talk about joining. If not, you'll still leave with clarity on what's holding you back.

Is this a motivation programme?

No. The whole point is to stop depending on motivation or panic. You'll build a practical execution system and the self-trust that comes from following through.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The outcomes above are what the programme aims for. What you get out of it depends on the actions you take during and after the seven weeks.
